## 5. Important Things to Consider Before Playing

To maintain a safe, transparent, and trouble-free experience on **New Port**, review the following practical checklist before registering or participating in any activity:

1. **Verify the Official Domain First:** Always confirm you are accessing the legitimate platform URL before entering login credentials or personal details, avoiding unverified mirror links.
2. **Audit Promotional Terms & Turnover Rules:** Never opt into a bonus based solely on headline numbers. Check minimum deposit thresholds, eligible game categories, expiration windows, and playthrough requirements in full.
3. **Confirm Legal Age and Jurisdictional Compliance:** Online gaming services strictly enforce minimum age regulations and regional restrictions. Verify that participation is permitted in your location and prepare valid identification if account verification (KYC) is required.
4. **Review Individual Game Rules:** Payout structures and feature rules vary significantly between providers and titles. Spend a minute reading the built-in help screen of any new game.
5. **Set Clear Time and Budget Boundaries:** Approach digital gaming strictly as paid entertainment. Establish fixed session limits and spending caps beforehand, and utilize available platform cooling-off or self-limitation tools whenever needed.
